# Learning Flow

## Fase 1 — Understanding & Analysing Curriculum (1–5)
Dosen-led pada 1–2, sesi dipandu mahasiswa pada 3–4, workshop kurikulum pelatihan pada 5.

## Fase 2 — Training Curriculum Blueprint (6–8)
Training Needs Analysis → Curriculum Blueprint → Curriculum Blueprint Forum/Defense.

## Fase 3 — Trends & Innovation (9–13)
STEM → AI/GenAI → ML/CV/Data → Virtual Lab/AR/VR → Education 5.0/Future Trends. Seluruhnya dipandu mahasiswa dengan strategi aktif.

## Fase 4 — Final Training Curriculum Design (14–16)
Full curriculum design → alignment audit/peer review → final pitch/oral defense.
